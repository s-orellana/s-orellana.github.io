I am a neuroscientist researching brain development and the origins of psychiatric disorders.

I recently finished a PhD in Computational Neuroscience at the University of Cambridge working with Prof. Ed Bullmore, Prof. Petra Vértes and Dr. Richard Bethlehem. In my work I have touched on psychology, medicine, psychiatry, developmental neuroscience, neuroimaging, computer science and complex-network theory.

This site is where I keep my research themes, publications, press coverage and training.
